Hi

After 7,254 meetings, I'm trying one with no agenda. Every Wednesday I keep three 45-minute slots open. No pitch, nothing to prepare.

Who I am

I'm András. Originally from Transylvania, now in Budapest. I started as a freelancer, studied media design, and for the past six years I've run Stereogram, a nine-person design studio. UX/UI, Framer sites, branding, the usual.

Now I'm changing how the studio works. We keep some of our client work, and more of my time goes into our own products, starting with Tendo and Unwritten. I've also taught design to career changers at UI Academy for six years and I've been a mentor at Krea for three.

I have little to no idea how the products will go. I'm posting about it anyway.

Why this exists

It's hard to meet new people once you're deep in the grind, corporate or agency. Most of my conversations for the last six years had a client, a brief or an invoice attached.

This is the opposite. You get an honest outside view from someone who's made a lot of these mistakes with real money. I get to meet people I wouldn't otherwise meet. Worst case, one of us learns something.
