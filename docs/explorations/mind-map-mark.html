A mark for mind-map

Five candidate marks in a micrographic style: hairlines, ticks, tiny readouts. Each one is drawn from the map itself, so it changes as the map grows: the number of entries, how many are published versus still ideas, and how many connections there are. The one accent colour, terracotta, is the moving part, which matches its role in the app as the colour of "now". Use the controls to grow the map and compare how each mark scales.

Today's map: the Instagram reel, two YouTube videos and five ideas, with two connections. Sizes shown: 144 px (detail), 48 px, the app bar's 18 px next to the wordmark, and a 16 px browser tab. Hairlines stay one pixel at every size (non-scaling strokes), which keeps the micrographic feel when the mark is large.
